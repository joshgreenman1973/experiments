The curb is on your right. Every turn here is made with the wheel all the way over, and the car's turning circle sets how tight that is. The tightest gap assumes a perfect driver who starts 3 feet out and ends 8 inches from the curb; real parking needs slack, which is why the lesson uses one and a half car lengths.
