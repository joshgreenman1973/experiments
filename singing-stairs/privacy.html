Privacy Policy

Singing Stairs for iPhone · Last updated 23 August 2026

Singing Stairs collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code of any kind.

Singing Stairs is an ear-training app for young children. It was written for one five-year-old and then made available to everyone else. It was built so that a grown-up handing a phone to a child does not have to wonder what the app is doing. What follows is the complete and literal answer.

The app has no network access

Singing Stairs contains no networking code. It never contacts a server — not ours, not anyone's. Every game, every piano sample and every drawing is bundled inside the app and runs entirely on the device. This is true whether the phone is online or in airplane mode; the app behaves identically either way.

Because nothing leaves the device, there is nothing for us to receive, see, store, sell or lose. We could not tell you who has installed the app or what anyone does in it.

The microphone

Two of the games — Sing together and Sing it back — listen to singing so the app can work out which note is being sung and show it climbing the staircase. This is the only reason the app ever asks for the microphone.

  • iOS asks your permission the first time one of those two games is opened. You may refuse. Everything else in the app — the other seven games and the whole playground — works normally without the microphone.
  • While one of those games is open, incoming audio is examined in memory, a few thousandths of a second at a time, purely to estimate the pitch. The app is asking one question: what frequency is this?
  • Audio is never recorded, never written to storage and never transmitted. There is no recording feature in the app. Each fragment of sound is discarded as soon as the next one arrives.
  • Listening stops when you leave the game.

No speech recognition is performed, and the app has no way to tell whose voice it is hearing or what words are being said. It only measures pitch.

What is saved on the device

The app remembers a few settings so it works the same way next time: which letters are awake, whether sharps and flats are shown, letter names or solfège, the chosen octave, and the number of stars earned. These are stored in the app's own private storage on your device using the standard iOS settings mechanism.

They never leave the phone, they are not backed up to any service of ours, and they contain no personal information — there is nowhere in Singing Stairs to type a name, an age, an email address or anything else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

This app is intended for children, so it was built to the simplest possible standard: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. There is no registration, no profile, no messaging, no user-generated content, no social feature, no advertising, no in-app purchase, and no link that leads out of the app to a website or a store.

Because no personal information is ever collected, there is none to disclose to a parent, correct, or delete — beyond deleting the app itself, which removes everything.

No tracking, no advertising, no analytics

Singing Stairs does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not ask for permission to track, and does not include any analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or advertising SDK. The app is not linked against any third-party library. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, the camera, the calendar, health data, or any other protected information — the microphone is the only permission it ever asks for.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
