Singing Stairs — Support

Help for grown-ups

Something not working, or a question about a game? Write to josh.greenman@gmail.com and you will get a real reply from the person who made the app.

What the app is

Singing Stairs teaches a young child to hear pitch. A staircase climbs the night sky from low C to high C, one letter per step, and the letters are characters with eyes and mouths. Tap any letter and it sings its true note. Nine games sit on top of that one idea. Nothing is scored, nothing is timed, and nothing can be done wrong.

Common questions

Where are the grown-up settings?

Press and hold the Grown-ups button in the bottom right for about a second and a half. The long press is deliberate — it keeps small fingers out. Inside you can turn sharps and flats on, switch letter names to solfège, put individual letters to sleep to make the games smaller for a younger child, wake everyone again, or start over.

The app is not hearing my child sing

  • Check that the microphone is allowed: Settings → Singing Stairs → Microphone. Only the two singing games use it.
  • The level meter on screen shows what the app is hearing. If it moves, the microphone is working and the issue is pitch, not permission.
  • Singing in a different octave is fine — the app judges the letter, not the height, and will say so ("That's it, in your low voice!").
  • Humming works better than singing words. So does being close to the phone in a quiet room.

The notes sound too high or too low for my child

Use the Low / Middle / High buttons on the Pick-a-game screen. They move the whole staircase to a different octave and play a short preview each time you tap. There is a second copy of the control inside Sing it back, where a child's own vocal range matters most.

My child is too young for nine games

Start in the playground — the first card. Eight pieces of equipment, each carrying one musical idea, with nothing to get right. The slide goes down and so do the notes; the swing goes higher and so does the pitch. You can also put letters to sleep in the grown-ups corner so the games use only three or four notes.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on the device, offline, always. See the privacy policy — the app has no networking code at all.

Is my child's singing recorded?

No. Sound is examined in memory to estimate pitch and immediately discarded. There is no recording feature and nothing is ever saved or sent anywhere.

Is there anything to buy inside the app?

No. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no advertising and no links out of the app. You buy it once and that is all of it.

How do I get rid of the stars and start again?

Grown-ups corner → Start over.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Reporting a problem

Email josh.greenman@gmail.com. It helps to say which game, what you expected, what happened instead, and which iPhone and iOS version you are on.
