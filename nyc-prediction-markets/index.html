NY prediction markets tracker

Every active prediction market touching New York City or New York State — elections, government, policy, the economy, transit, crime and public health — pulled from Polymarket, Kalshi and Manifold every six hours and consolidated here. Markets are matched by place plus civic context, so new races and candidates surface automatically. Sports, entertainment and daily weather are excluded.
