Paid on public work

Contractors on public construction in New York must file a certified payroll every week: who worked, in what trade, at what hourly rate. The state Department of Labor publishes those filings. The city comptroller publishes the wage each trade is owed. This puts the two side by side for every project in the five boroughs in 2026: — worker-weeks across — projects and — contractors.
