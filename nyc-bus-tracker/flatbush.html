Up and Down

Bus speeds on Flatbush Avenue in Brooklyn before, during and after the center-running bus lanes between Livingston Street and Grand Army Plaza opened in November 2026.

The first week

Figures are for weekdays, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. The lanes are new, so these are early readings from one week of service.

    Day by day

    B41 speed on the rebuilt mile, Livingston Street to Grand Army Plaza, from GPS positions of buses in the MTA feed. Speeds include time at stops and lights. Each day’s 12 hours from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. count equally.

    Southbound, toward Kings Plaza Northbound, toward Downtown Brooklyn

    The shaded area is construction, which ran from April 28 to Nov. 6. Hollow points are weekends and days when schools were closed. The tracker’s archive begins April 28, 2026, the day the second phase of construction started, but it caught too few hours of most days before July to plot. The lower chart is the rest of Flatbush, Grand Army Plaza to Kings Plaza.

    How these numbers are made

    The concept, the choice of data and the layout of this page came from its author, a human. The code was written mainly by Anthropic’s Claude, under close direction. The tracker’s speeds on the stretch were checked month by month against the MTA’s own segment speeds. Speeds are estimates made from GPS positions, not official MTA numbers, and AI-built pipelines can still go wrong. Please flag anything that looks off.
