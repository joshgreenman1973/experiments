NYC BUS TRACKER: DATA DICTIONARY (method version 2, October 2026)
Copied into data/downloads/ by scripts/export_csv.mjs with every refresh.
Project: https://github.com/joshgreenman1973/experiments/tree/main/nyc-bus-tracker
Method changes, dated: CHANGELOG.txt in this folder.

TWO KINDS OF DATA LIVE HERE. Do not mix them.

  A. SPEED AND BUSES: the tracker's own estimates from GPS positions in the
     MTA BusTime feed. Not official MTA figures.
  B. RIDERSHIP: the MTA's own numbers, republished. Passenger-counter
     boardings (data.ny.gov fvdm-uavx) and paid fare taps (kv7t-n8in through
     2024, gxb3-akrn from 2025).

HOW SPEED IS MEASURED (applies to every speed column)
  The collector records every bus in the feed about a dozen times over six
  minutes, at 9 and 39 minutes past every hour, 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern.
  A bus seen in two consecutive snapshots of the same burst gives one reading:
  straight-line distance between its two GPS positions over the time between
  its two GPS timestamps. Dropped: readings where either position is flagged
  "layover", readings where the bus's GPS timestamp did not advance (no new
  position), and readings of 60 mph or more.
    with_stops   total distance / total time for a route in an hour, time
                 stopped at stops and lights included. THE HEADLINE MEASURE.
    moving       mean of readings at 0.5 mph or more. The site's measure before
                 October 2026; leaves out time stopped, so it reads higher.
    stopped_share  share of reading time under 0.5 mph.
  A route-hour needs 2 minutes of readings for with_stops and 3 readings for
  moving.

HOW FIGURES ARE WEIGHTED
  Hours are Eastern clock hours 6 through 22 (6:00 a.m. to 10:59 p.m.), 17 in
  all. System and borough speeds use local, limited and Select Bus Service
  routes, each counting once; express routes (BM, BxM, QM, SIM, X) are their
  own group, and temporary shuttles (routes absent from the MTA's published
  timetable, such as T102 or B90) are left out. For a week or month, each
  hour of each day type is averaged over that period's days, then the 17 hours
  are averaged with equal weight. Weekdays and weekends are always separate;
  "index" columns blend them 5 to 2.
  Day types: Saturday and Sunday are weekend. A weekday is "holiday" (left out
  of weekday figures) when it is a major holiday (New Year's, Memorial Day,
  Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas) or when
  fewer than 85% as many routes ran at midday as on nearby weekdays.

COMPARABILITY: READ THIS BEFORE COMPARING ANY TWO PERIODS
  A week is comparable when all 7 days are in and every one of the 17 hours
  was caught on at least 3 weekdays and at least 1 weekend day, except that up
  to 2 hours per day type may be missed. A missed hour is filled by assuming it
  moved like the rest of that period's day relative to its usual level (the
  mean of the usual hourly levels plus the period's average deviation from them
  over the hours it has); a fully caught period is unchanged by this. The
  filled hours are listed in weekly.json (wd.filledHours, we.filledHours).
  Weekend 8 p.m. was often missed before October 2026. A month needs 8
  weekdays and 3 weekend days per hour and must be over. Incomplete day
  types leave the speed columns blank rather than averaging whatever hours
  were caught. Before late June 2026 the collector caught only some hours of
  most days, so few spring weeks are comparable; June 2026 was mostly evening
  only. Oct. 1 to 6, 2026, caught 3 to 5 hours a day.

---------------------------------------------------------------- daily-system.csv
One row per day. Speeds here average only the hours caught that day, so a
day with evening hours only reads fast; compare days only when
hours_collected is 17.
  day_type, day_type_reason   weekday / weekend / holiday, and why
  hours_collected             of the 17 window hours, those with 6+ snapshots
  routes_running_midday       regular routes with a bus in service 10 a.m.-4 p.m.
  buses_in_service            buses in the feed not on layover, mean per hour
  buses_in_service_regular_routes  the same, timetable routes only
  trips_scheduled             trips the MTA timetable has under way, mean per
                              hour caught (from Sept. 6, 2026)

------------------------------------------- weekly-system.csv, monthly-system.csv
One row per ISO week or calendar month.
  coverage_pct         share of day-hours caught (17 x days)
  comparable           see COMPARABILITY. Compare only rows where this is true.
  weekday_complete, weekend_complete   each day type's hours all caught enough
  index_*              weekday and weekend blended 5:2
  weekday_*_mph        weekday, all 17 hours equal
  weekday_6am / am_peak / midday / pm_peak / evening   hour bands: 6 a.m.;
                       7-10 a.m.; 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; 4-7 p.m.; 7-11 p.m.
  weekday_buses_in_service   mean buses on the road per hour, weekdays
  weekday_trips_scheduled    mean timetable trips under way per hour
  weekday_service_ratio      buses on the road on regular routes / timetable trips
                       under way. It ran 0.96-0.98 in September 2026. A trip
                       not run lowers it; so does a bus whose GPS isn't
                       reporting to BusTime, and buses running between trips
                       raise it. An indicator to watch over time, not the MTA's
                       service-delivered figure.
  mta_style_*          all regular routes including express, the way the MTA's
                       hourly route-speed file is averaged; for comparison only.

------------------------------------------------------------ weekly-borough.csv
  group                M, B (Brooklyn), Bx, Q, S; X = express routes
  weekday_routes       routes with a comparable weekday week, averaged plainly

---------------------------------------------- weekly-routes.csv, monthly-routes.csv
One row per route per period it ran.
  kind                 local, limited, sbs, express, shuttle (from the MTA timetable)
  weekdays_seen        weekdays (not holidays) the route had a bus in service
  weekday_comparable   system collection complete for weekdays AND the route
                       ran on at least 3 weekdays (8 for a month). Compare only
                       rows where this is true.
  weekday_hours_running  window hours in which the route ran
  route speeds         every hour the route ran counts equally
  weekday_buses_in_service  route buses on the road, mean over all 17 hours
                       (zero in hours it did not run)
  weekday_passengers_per_bus  mean of the live passenger-counter estimate on
                       buses that report one; an indicator of crowding, not a count

---------------------------------------------------- route-ridership-monthly.csv
One row per route per complete calendar month (source B).
  counter_boardings, counter_avg_weekday   automatic passenger counters
  fare_taps, fare_taps_avg_weekday   paid OMNY and MetroCard entries,
                       including free transfers. Q52+ and Q53+ are reported
                       together by the MTA; both rows carry the pair's total.
  counter_to_taps_ratio   counters / taps, weekday averages
  network_change_this_month   true where the Queens bus network redesign
                       (June 29 and Aug. 31, 2025; QM routes Sept. 2, 2025)
                       changed the route that month. Do not compare a route
                       across that line.
  Weekday averages divide Monday-Friday boardings by calendar weekdays in the
  month; holidays count as weekdays. Neither series is a full count of riders:
  taps miss riders who don't pay (the MTA put local-bus fare evasion at 49.6% in
  the second quarter of 2026, data.ny.gov uv5h-dfhp), and counters miss riders
  on buses without a working counter. Systemwide the counters ran only 6% to
  13% above taps in June-August 2026. A route's ratio can jump when counter
  coverage changes, so a single route's counter trend can move for reasons that
  have nothing to do with riders. January 2026 taps are blank: the MTA file has
  near-zero taps for Jan. 10-15.

------------------------------------------------------------ mta-comparison.csv
  mta_headline_mph     MTA Bus Speeds (cudb-vcni): all routes, all hours,
                       weighted by miles run. The figure the MTA reports.
  mta_route_hour_equal_mph   MTA hourly route speeds (r6db-kkzj) built the
                       tracker's way: local, limited and SBS routes each once,
                       hours 6-22 equal, weekday and weekend blended 5:2.
  ours_with_stops_mph, ours_moving_mph   the tracker's index, same weighting
  nowcast_mta_headline_mph   forecast of the MTA headline for a month the MTA
                       has not yet published: last published headline times the
                       tracker's own change. Recorded before publication and
                       scored after, in data/mta/nowcasts.json.

-------------------------------------------------------------------- events.csv
  kind   holiday (or service cut), gap (fewer than 12 of 17 hours caught, or no
         data), weather (0.5 in. of rain or 1 in. of snow at the Central Park
         station, NOAA GHCN-Daily USW00094728), method, network

------------------------------------------------------- stops-hourly-june2026.csv
One row per bus stop, June 2026 (source B). 13,351 stops.
  HOUR COLUMNS ARE MONTH TOTALS, NOT DAILY AVERAGES.
  wd_total_h0 .. wd_total_h23    boardings summed across all weekdays of the month
  we_total_h0 .. we_total_h23    boardings summed across all weekend days
  weekday_days, weekend_days     the divisors (22 and 8 for June 2026)
  month_boardings/alightings     whole-month totals across all 30 days
  m_to_subway                    straight-line meters to the nearest of the 496
                                 stations in the MTA Subway Stations file, which
                                 includes the 21 Staten Island Railway stations.
                                 38.3% of stops are beyond 800 m counting the SIR,
                                 41.5% without it.
  in_crz                         1 if inside the MTA congestion relief zone (the
                                 Central Business District polygon, minus the
                                 excluded roadways: FDR Drive, West Side
                                 Highway/Route 9A, Battery Park Underpass, Hugh L.
                                 Carey Tunnel connections)
  Juneteenth (Friday, June 19, 2026) ran holiday service but counts as a weekday
  here. 298 stop IDs (0.63% of June boardings) had no GTFS location and are
  excluded.

-------------------------------------------------------------- route-classes.csv
  bus_lane_share   share of the route's shape length within about 60 m of a
                   marked bus lane or busway (New York City Department of
                   Transportation, ycrg-ses3), both densified to 15 m. Proximity
                   does not prove the route uses the lane.
  cbd_relation     "In CBD" / "Crossing CBD" from the MTA's list (cgzt-smqf), or
                   "Outside CBD".
  Compare each group with its own past: bus lanes go where traffic is worst.

WHAT NOT TO DO
  - Don't compare rows where comparable (or weekday_comparable) is false.
  - Don't compare tracker speeds before October 2026 editions with these: every
    day was recomputed, and older published numbers used moving speed.
  - Don't read a single route's counter ridership trend without its taps and
    ratio beside it.
  - Don't treat buses_in_service against trips_scheduled as an MTA
    service-delivered figure; it is an indicator for trends.
