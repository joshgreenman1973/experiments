01 — Why this matters
A small group of drivers accounts for a large share of camera tickets
Speed is a major factor in whether a crash is fatal. A small share of drivers account for much of the camera-detected speeding, and the costs show up on everyone's insurance.

A small group of repeat offenders accounts for a large share of the camera-detected speeding. The Dangerous Vehicle Abatement Program (DVAP) was written for precisely this group — any car with 15+ school-zone speed tickets in 12 months is eligible to be booted or impounded.

Speeding drives crashes, crashes drive insurance claims, and insurance is pooled risk. New York drivers now pay some of the highest auto-insurance premiums in the country — and a measurable portion of that tracks to the drivers who crash most, not the typical driver.
Lower-risk drivers help cover the cost of higher-risk ones. Emergency rooms, ambulance runs, disability claims, lost-work days — every collision is a bill that eventually lands on a premium, a tax, or a hospital ledger.
NYC avg full-coverage premium ~$4,100/yr (2025, Bankrate); portion attributable to higher-risk driving is an estimate — see methodology.
