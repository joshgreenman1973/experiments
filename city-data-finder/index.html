Every Friday morning a Claude scheduled task searches the web for city data projects published or announced in the past week (maps, tools, datasets, charts and analysis) and adds 8 to 15 of them here, New York City first. The place and kind labels come from that scan and can be wrong; a handful were corrected by hand in October 2026.

Each scan is saved as its own file in city-data-finder/data.
