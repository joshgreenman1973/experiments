Every card plots the same 13-quarter frame — Q2 2023 through Q2 2026. Solid lines are verified from public sources. Dashed placeholders mark series still being assembled: as real observations land each month and quarter, the line fills in.

Four rules, applied to every item:
  • Real or nothing. Every printed level ties to a named public source with a retrieval date. Historical quarters labeled "est." are back-filled from published archives — flagged so you can tell real observations from the back-fill.
  • Same quarterly frame. Q2 2023 → Q2 2026, 13 quarters, on every card. Gaps stay as gaps.
  • Same spec each quarter. Same tariff, same panel, same collection rule. No silent sample changes.
  • Work shown. Each quarter's values and their source notes live in readings.json in the repo.

Full methodology

Why quarterly?

Monthly reads too noisy for some series (gas, rent), and quarterly events (toll hikes, water rate resets, Broadway seasons) fit cleanly on a quarterly axis. We still collect monthly where possible and roll up to the quarter's average (or quarter-ending value for step series like tolls).

Three kinds of series, three kinds of math

  • Published tariffs (6 items): subway, toll, Citi Bike, water, ConEd, taxi. Quarterly value = rate in effect at quarter end.
  • Public indices & averages (3 items): AAA gas, StreetEasy rent, BLS food CPI. Quarterly value = arithmetic mean of the three monthly prints.
  • Fixed-panel surveys (5 items): pizza, bagel, coffee, Uber benchmark, family dinner. Quarterly value = mean of the three monthly survey means (or single collection for family dinner).
  • Broadway (1 item): per-season value held flat across a season's quarters until the next season closes.

Back-fill provenance

Pre-Q2 2026 values on the verified cards come from these archives: MTA fare and toll press releases, AAA / FRED monthly averages (series APUS12A7471A), NYC Water Board annual rate schedules, ConEd residential rate history (bundled averages published annually), Broadway League season statistics, NYC TLC tariff press releases. Each historical quarter's source note is in readings.json. Gas and ConEd historical quarters are approximate — the shape is right, the month-to-month precision is not. Marked est. where that applies.

What this doesn't measure

Wages, income-adjusted burden, tip inflation, shrinkflation, rent-stabilized contracts, or the full cost of owning a car. Directional gauge, not a cost-of-living index.
