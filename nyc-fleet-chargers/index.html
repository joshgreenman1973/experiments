This map against the city's October 2026 PDF

The Department of Citywide Administrative Services publishes a picture of its network as of October 2026 with counts but no addresses. The open dataset with the addresses was last updated in May, so this map is short of the October count, mostly in ordinary Level 2 plugs.

And please read this AI caution. The idea and the choice of sources are the author's (a human). The code, the data pull and the geocoding were done by Anthropic's Claude Code under close direction. Quality control on this project: every count on this page was recomputed from the city's file; every published coordinate was checked against the city's ZIP code boundaries and re-geocoded through the city's own address search, which turned up two misplaced sites that are now corrected; each of the 307 fixed stations the city published without coordinates was placed by a written rule, and each placement is listed in the methodology. The risks of using AI remain, and red flags are welcome.
