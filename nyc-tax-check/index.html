Tax proposals and you

 

Describe your household and the page runs it through the ten state and city income tax proposals we found in New York, then lines them up by what each would do to your family's bill. The tax rules come from PolicyEngine US, the open-source tax and benefit model built by PolicyEngine, whose model can run most of them directly. It covers full-year New York State residents whose income is wages and who take the standard deduction, in the city or elsewhere in the state. Two of the proposals change only New York City's income tax, so they matter only to city residents.

Credit. Every tax rule, rate, threshold and credit formula on this page comes from PolicyEngine US (version 2.145.7), built by PolicyEngine. PolicyEngine runs in Python, so this page uses a JavaScript translation of the parts that apply to New York wage earners, shared with the tax levers calculator. The translation was run side by side with PolicyEngine on 1,620 made-up households under 19 scenarios, including each of PolicyEngine's New York bill reforms, and all 430,920 values agreed within a dollar, apart from rounding of a few dollars at incomes in the tens of millions. Where a proposal goes beyond what PolicyEngine models, its row says so. Any mistakes are this page's, not PolicyEngine's.
