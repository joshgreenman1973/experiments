    Why the map stops at 2020. In 2021, NYC Buildings migrated most alteration filings from the legacy system to DOB NOW. The new system no longer publishes the free-text job description field, so there's no reliable way to identify apartment combinations in it. Post-2020 combinations still happen — they're just no longer separately identifiable in the public data.
