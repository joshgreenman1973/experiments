# Women & Guns in America — Data & Sources Ledger

**Purpose:** This is the editable companion to `index.html`. It lists *every* data point in the
story, the exact figure as published, the precise source (with the verbatim supporting language
where available), and a verification status. Anyone — editor, reader, fact-checker — should be
able to trace each number back to its origin and confirm it independently.

**Last fact-checked:** June 2026 (four independent source-verification passes).
**Maintainer note:** When you change a number in `index.html`, change it here too, and update the
status line. Never let the two drift apart.

**Status legend**
- ✅ **Confirmed** — the cited source states the figure (or it is a sound, disclosed derivation).
- 🟡 **Confirmed w/ caveat** — figure is right but needs a year/scope/attribution note (see NOTE).
- 🔧 **Corrected** — the original draft had an error; the published figure now reflects the fix.
- ⚠️ **Estimate / contested** — defensible but methodology-sensitive; attribute carefully, don't present as settled.

## Cross-cutting notes for editors

1. **Mixed-vintage chart (C4).** The women-by-group ownership chart combines a 2024 Gallup figure with 2017 Pew figures. The chart carries an explicit note; do not present the bars as a single-year snapshot.
2. **Odds ratios vs. risk.** D1, S4, SD4, SD5 are odds ratios from observational studies. "Roughly 5x / triples / 7x / doubles" are fair plain-language renderings, but they are associations, not proven causal multipliers.
3. **Advocacy sources.** CPRC (SD2, CU3) and Everytown/Giffords/VPC are advocacy or advocacy-aligned. Their *numbers* check out against their stated methods, but attribute them by name so readers can weigh the source.
4. **The "21x" comparison (C9).** Methodology-sensitive and previously fact-checked as "Half True" in a related form. Always attribute to Giffords/Grinshteyn-Hemenway and use the "die from gun violence" framing.
5. **Self-reported figures (CU2).** A Girl & A Gun's 40% growth is the organization's own unaudited number. Keep the "the organization reports" qualifier.
